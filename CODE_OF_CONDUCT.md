# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

PathShelf welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.
Project spaces should remain respectful, technically constructive, and safe.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Giving actionable feedback about code and ideas rather than people.
- Respecting different experience levels, communication styles, and constraints.
- Disclosing conflicts of interest and correcting mistakes promptly.
- Keeping private reports and personal information confidential.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and
sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, release discussions, and
other spaces where a person is representing PathShelf.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an immediate public moderation need, use GitHub's report and block controls.
For a private report, use the repository's
[private reporting channel](../../security/advisories/new) and prefix the title
with `Code of Conduct`. Reports are reviewed by maintainers who are not named in
the complaint whenever that is possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently remove a participant. Decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately, and protect reporter confidentiality.

No one may retaliate against a person who makes a good-faith report.
